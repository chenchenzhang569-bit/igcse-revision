Similarity

  • If two objects are similar, that means that they are the exact same shape but different in size
  • All corresponding lengths must be in the same ratio
  • AAA (Angle – Angle – Angle) Rule: All the corresponding angles of the triangles must be equal.

Geometrical Terms

Congruent

  • Two shapes that have the identical shape and size

Scale Factor

  • A number that indicates how much a shape has been enlarged or reduced in size

Vertex

  • An angular point where two or more lines or edges meet

Perpendicular Bisector

  • A line that bisects a line segment in two equal parts and makes a angle of 90 degrees at the point of intersection

Parallel Lines

  • Two or more lines that are always the same distance apart and never intersect

Similar

  • They have the same shape but different sizes.

  • All corresponding angles are equal.

  • All corresponding side lengths are in the same ratio (scale factor)

Acute Angle

  • An angle less than 90°

Obtuse Angle

  • An angle greater than 90° but less than 180°

Reflex Angle

  • An angle greater than 180° but less than 360°

Interior Angles

  • The angles inside a polygon or shape

Exterior Angles

  • The angles outside a polygon, formed by extending one side.

  • For any polygon, exterior angles add up to 360°

Right Angle

  • An angle of 90°.

  • Shown with a small square in diagrams

Bearings

  • A direction measured:

    • Clockwise

    • From North

    • Written as a three-digit number (e.g., 045°, 120°, 270°)

Perpendicular

  • Two lines are perpendicular if they meet at a right angle (90°)

Parallel

  • Two lines are parallel if:

    • They are always the same distance apart.

    • They never meet, no matter how far extended

Line

  • A straight path that:

    • Extends infinitely in both directions.

    • Has no thickness

Point

  • A precise location in space.

  • Has no size, no length, and no width

Congruence

  • If two objects are congruent, that means that they are identical. Same shape and same size

  • SSS (Side – Side – Side) Rule: All three sides of the triangles must be equal

  • RHS (Right angle – Hypotenuse – Side) rule :

  • There must be two right-angled triangles

  • SAS (Side – Angle – Side) rule:

  • ASA (Angle – Side – Angle) rule: The sides adjacent to the equal angle angles must be of the same length.

Angle Notation:

  • Angles are represented with this symbol - ∠\angle∠

  • Its written like ∠ABC\angle {A}{B}{C}∠ABC with the middle letter (B) being the vertex

Triangles

Equilateral Triangle

  • All three sides are equal in length.

  • All three angles are equal (each 60°).

  • Perfectly symmetrical.

Isosceles Triangle

  • Has two equal sides.

  • The angles opposite the equal sides are also equal.

  • Has one line of symmetry.

  • Useful for angle-finding questions using the rule

  • Base angles in an isosceles triangle are equal

Scalene Triangle

  • No sides are equal.

  • No angles are equal.

  • Irregular shape with no symmetry.

Right-Angled Triangle

  • Contains one right angle (90°).

  • The side opposite the right angle is the hypotenuse (the longest side).

  • Used for:

    • Pythagoras’ theorem

    • Trigonometry (sin, cos, tan)

Quadrilaterals

Rectangle

  • Opposite sides are parallel and equal

  • All internal angles are 90°

  • Diagonals bisect each other

  • Diagonals are equal in length

Parallelogram

  • Opposite sides are parallel and equal

  • Opposite angles are equal

  • Diagonals are not necessarily equal

  • No right angles unless it's a rectangle

  • diagonals bisect each other

Rhombus

  • A parallelogram with all sides equal

  • Opposite angles are equal

  • Diagonals bisect each other at 90°, and they bisect opposite angles

Trapezium

  • One pair of sides is parallel

Kite

  • Two pairs of adjacent sides are equal

  • Diagonals are perpendicular to each other

  • One pair of opposite angles is equal (those between the unequal sides)

  • One diagonal is bisected (the one joining equal angles

Square

  • Four equal sides and four right angles

  • Opposite sides are parallel

  • Diagonals that are equal in length and bisect each other at right angles

  • Diagonals bisect opposite angles

Construction

  • Constructing triangles:

Step 1 : Draw the base line using a ruler

Step 2 : Using a compass, measure the desired length on a ruler and draw an arc

Step 3 : Similarly use the compass to measure the 2nd side and draw an arc

Step 4 : Connect the ends of the baseline to the point where the arc intersect.

Scale Drawings :

Symmetry and Nets

  • Line of Symmetry: Divides a two-dimensional shape into two congruent (identical) shapes

  • Plane of Symmetry: Divides a three-dimensional shape into two congruent solid shapes

  • Rotational Symmetry: The number of times a shape looks the same during a 360° rotation about its center

Bearings

  • The bearing of a point B from another point A is:
    • An angle measured from the north at A.
    • In a clockwise direction.
    • Written as three-figure number (i.e. from 000° to 360°)

How to Calculate Bearings (from the example above)

  • Step 1 — Identify the starting point

    • Bearing of A from B” means:

      • Start at B

      • Look towards A

  • Step 2 — Draw or identify the North line

    • At point B, draw a vertical line pointing up — this is North.

    • Bearings are always measured from the North line.

  • Step 3 — Bearings must be measured clockwise

    • This is the most important rule.

    • Always rotate clockwise from the North line until you reach the line pointing to the other point.

  • Step 4 — Look at the angle given in the diagram

    • The diagram shows 108°, but this angle is measured anticlockwise from the North line down to line BA.

    BUT bearings are not measured anticlockwise — only clockwise.

  • Step 5 — Use the full circle to find the clockwise angle

    The full turn around a point is 360°.

    Since the diagram gives the anticlockwise angle:

    Clockwise angle=360∘−108∘=252∘\text{Clockwise angle} = 360^\circ - 108^\circ = 252^\circClockwise angle=360∘−108∘=252∘

  • Final Answer = 252
